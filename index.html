About us

From humble beginnings.

Don Andres started with one boat and the twenty footers that fed the island. Today the de Jongh family runs three ships, a chassis fleet and offices on Bonaire and Curaçao. The job has not changed. Keep Bonaire supplied.

1980sA family business begins on the pier in Kralendijk.
2019Doña Luisa I opens the Rio Haina route, replacing the Venezuelan produce boats.
2024ABC Direct from Trinidad with Gulf Shipping. Two days to Curaçao.
TodayThree vessels, own trucks and a side loader. Over 35 years of twenty footers.
