Since the twenty footers

One boat on the Kralendijk pier. Then the containers came, and the de Jongh family grew with them. Three ships, a chassis fleet, offices on two islands. The job is the same as day one. Keep Bonaire supplied.

1980sA family business starts on the pier in Kralendijk.
2019Doña Luisa I opens Rio Haina, replacing the Venezuelan produce boats.
